# BarcodeCraft notes

- 2026-11-14: started from BarCraft, a barcode offshoot of LabelCraft made earlier (only on the web servers until then). The goal (the user's): one barcode core for every Craft app, information pulled and pushed between the apps, barcodes inserted as pictures on any sheet in any app; first use: ShiftCraft contact cards with QR codes.
- `js/craft-barcode.js` (window.CraftBarcode): the 71 types of BarCraft's catalogue (`{ id, name, sample, kind: linear | 2d | postal, bcid, group }`). `make()` builds an SVG in mm: a white background, the code with its quiet zone inside the size given (10 modules each side of linear codes, 4 round 2D codes), linear codes stretched to the height with the text under them. bwip-js draws a module as 1 unit in linear codes and 2 in 2D ones. QR codes are made with qrcode-generator in UTF-8: bwip-js's QR codes with accented letters did not decode (tested with OpenCV), even with the UTF-8 bytes and binarytext; the other 2D types get the UTF-8 bytes with binarytext. `png()` / `dataUrl()` draw the SVG at the DPI asked for. `vcard()` makes a vCard 3.0. `addSource(name, list)`: what the app offers to the window. Library: localStorage `craft-barcodes`, and logged in, AccountCraft `craft` / `barcodes` (merged by id, newest kept). `open()`: the Insert › Barcode window (kind, From <app>, what it holds, size, text, colour, QR error correction, live preview, Save to the library, Insert).
- Tested in headless Chrome: all 71 types draw with their samples; the window's From list filled a contact card and Insert returned it; a contact QR for "Zoë Ångström" read back exactly by OpenCV at 40 and 60 mm, 300 and 600 DPI, error correction L and M.
